# Pathfinder Product Philosophy

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Canonical Product Artifact

## Purpose

This document explains the enduring philosophy behind Pathfinder. It
complements the Vision Lock by describing the beliefs that guide product
design, engineering, user experience, and roadmap decisions. It is
intentionally more stable than the PRD and more explanatory than the
Product Principles.

## Foundational Belief

Pathfinder does not help users manage a list of tasks.

Pathfinder helps users understand and continuously navigate their Route
through reentry.

This distinction defines the product.

## Why Route-First?

Reentry is not experienced as isolated tasks. Employment,
identification, transportation, housing, supervision, finances, health,
and family obligations influence one another.

A Route acknowledges those relationships and presents a coherent
sequence instead of a disconnected collection of work.

## Explainability Before Prediction

Users should understand *why* an action appears where it does.

Pathfinder favors transparent reasoning over opaque recommendations.
Trust is strengthened when users can see why an action comes next and
what it unlocks.

## Deterministic Where It Matters

The Route Engine owns sequencing.

Artificial intelligence enhances understanding by interpreting
documents, summarizing information, and producing explanations, but it
never replaces deterministic routing.

## Trust Through Confirmed Facts

Every Route begins with trustworthy information.

Proposed Facts are reviewed before becoming Confirmed Facts. Provenance
accompanies route-affecting information so users understand where it
originated.

## Calm Interaction

Complexity exists within the system, not the interface.

The Route View progressively reveals information and emphasizes today's
work without overwhelming users with every dependency at once.

## User Control

Users remain in control of their information.

Sharing is explicit, transparent, and revocable. Pathfinder is not
designed as a surveillance platform or a hidden reporting tool.

## Product Discipline

New capabilities should strengthen one or more of the following:

-   Route creation
-   Route quality
-   Route explainability
-   Route trust
-   Route adaptation through Reroute

Features that distract from these goals should be deferred or rejected.

## Long-Term Philosophy

As Pathfinder evolves, technology, workflows, and integrations may
change. The philosophy does not.

The product succeeds when it consistently transforms complex,
interconnected circumstances into an understandable, trustworthy Route
that adapts as life changes.

## Relationship to Other Documents

-   The Documentation Constitution governs documentation.
-   The Vision Lock preserves immutable product identity.
-   The Glossary defines canonical terminology.
-   The Product Principles guide decision-making.
-   The PRD specifies product requirements.
-   Architecture documents describe implementation.

## Governance

Changes to this philosophy require an approved Product Decision.
Architectural consequences additionally require an Architecture Decision
Record (ADR).
