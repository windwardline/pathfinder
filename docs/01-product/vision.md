# Pathfinder Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Canonical Product Vision Document

## Purpose

This document explains the Pathfinder product from a strategic
perspective. It complements the Vision Lock by describing why Pathfinder
exists, who it serves, what makes it different, and how Release 1
delivers meaningful value. Stable definitions remain in the Vision Lock
and Glossary; this document references those foundations rather than
redefining them.

## Why Pathfinder Exists

Reentry is not a single problem. People returning to their communities
often navigate employment, housing, identification, transportation,
healthcare, supervision requirements, financial obligations, family
responsibilities, and deadlines simultaneously. These domains are
interconnected. Progress in one area frequently depends on actions
completed in another.

Most existing approaches organize information, referrals, or tasks.
Pathfinder addresses a different problem: helping users understand the
order in which actions unlock meaningful progress.

## Vision

Pathfinder enables justice-impacted individuals to move through reentry
with confidence by continuously generating an explainable Route that
adapts as life changes.

The product transforms complexity into clarity by answering three
questions:

-   What comes next?
-   Why does it come next?
-   What does it unlock?

## Target User

Release 1 focuses on justice-impacted adults during the first year after
release who are managing multiple interconnected obligations, goals,
barriers, and deadlines.

This intentionally narrow focus allows Pathfinder to validate the
Route-first model before expanding into additional workflows or
collaboration features.

## Product Experience

The user interacts with a calm, adaptive Route View rather than a
complex planning system.

Behind the experience:

Confirmed Facts → Dependency Graph → Route Engine → Route → Adaptive
Route View

Users primarily experience the Route. The supporting infrastructure
remains largely invisible.

When circumstances change, Pathfinder performs a Reroute that explains
the impact of those changes in understandable language.

## Strategic Differentiation

Pathfinder is not designed to replace case managers, legal
professionals, or community organizations.

Instead, it complements existing services by continuously sequencing
actions into an explainable Route built from confirmed user
circumstances.

The distinguishing capability is not artificial intelligence alone; it
is deterministic routing supported by trustworthy data, provenance, and
meaningful Reroute behavior.

## Release 1 Vision

Release 1 proves that Pathfinder can:

-   Capture structured information.
-   Confirm route-affecting facts.
-   Generate a deterministic Route.
-   Explain why each action comes next.
-   Preserve provenance.
-   Demonstrate meaningful Reroute after change.

## Long-Term Direction

Future releases may broaden supported workflows, collaboration, and
decision support, provided every capability strengthens the Route-first
philosophy and respects user control, privacy, and deterministic
sequencing.

## Success Measures

Pathfinder succeeds when users:

-   Understand what to do next.
-   Understand why it comes next.
-   Maintain trust in the Route.
-   Experience reduced cognitive load.
-   Adapt confidently when circumstances change.
