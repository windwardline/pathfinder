## Purpose

This is the de-identified evidence record for H1 through H4 in
[Validation Strategy](validation.md). The field protocol that would produce this
record is defined in [User Validation Plan](user-validation-plan.md). Automated
tests cannot complete this study. Only observed participant sessions may be
recorded as results.

Participant recruitment and facilitation are not available before the current
Demo Day milestone. The study is therefore outside the pre-demo critical path.
This scheduling constraint does not convert technical verification into human
product validation, and it does not authorize any comprehension, trust,
cognitive-load, or outcome claim. The protocol remains available for a future
partner-led study if access becomes feasible.

## Participant Safety and Data Rules

- Recruit through an ethical nonprofit or course process.
- Participation is voluntary and may stop at any time.
- Use fictional seeded scenarios; do not request personal reentry records.
- Record participant codes such as `P01`, never names, email addresses, case
  identifiers, supervision details, or document contents.
- Store only task outcomes, ratings, de-identified observations, and consent state.
- Do not place recordings or raw notes containing personal information in Git.

## Session Script

1. Explain that Pathfinder is a prototype and not legal advice or a risk score.
2. Load SD-001 and ask the participant to identify what comes next.
3. Ask why it comes next and what it unlocks without coaching.
4. Complete the Focus Action and ask the participant to explain the Reroute.
5. Show a Proposed Fact and ask whether it currently affects the Route.
6. Confirm it and ask what changed.
7. Repeat the comprehension task with an equivalent static checklist.
8. Collect confidence, ease, and perceived-complexity ratings from 1 through 5.

## Success Gates

- At least 80% identify the Focus Action without coaching.
- At least 80% correctly explain its reason and one unlock.
- At least 80% correctly interpret the Reroute.
- At least 80% understand that a Proposed Fact has no routing effect.
- Pathfinder shows no worse task accuracy than the equivalent checklist.
- Any cognitive-load or trust claim cites the observed sample size and uncertainty.
