# Read this out loud — Refactored 90s film

Record each numbered chunk on its own (2–3 takes each). Talk to ONE person across
a table. Slower than feels natural. Mean it. Leave 2 seconds of silence before and
after each chunk.

**Bold** = lean on these words. (…) = small pause.

---

**1 · Open** — quiet, honest, a little heavy
> For a lot of us, the day you come home, the world hands you a **list**. (…) Nobody hands you the **order**.

**2 · The trap** — pick up pace, matter-of-fact, let it build
> You need an ID to get a job, (…) an address to get the ID, (…) a job to keep the address. One wrong step, and it **all falls apart**. Reentry isn't a paperwork problem — it's a **sequencing** problem.

**3 · Turn** — warmer, a small breath of hope
> So we built the map we wish we'd had. (…) We call it **Pathfinder**.

**4 · The product** — clear, confident, showing something you're proud of
> It turns the facts of your life into one clear **Route** — one step at a time. Start with a state ID: the one move that **unlocks the next three**. Mark it done, and the Route **rebuilds itself** — showing exactly what changed, and why. (…) Real, working software.

**5 · The hinge** — slow down. This is the pivot. Let it land.
> But Pathfinder isn't just a **product**. (…) It's **proof**.

**6 · Refactored** — stand tall. This is your company. Confident, not loud.
> Proof of what we can build. I'm launching **Refactored** — a software company that hires formerly incarcerated engineers and competes on one thing: **excellence**. We don't lower the bar. (…) We raise people to **meet it**.

**7 · Close** — warm, certain, land the last line clean
> Hire us because we're among the **best** — the impact comes free. Because talent is **everywhere**; (…) the chance to prove it isn't. (…) **Refactored.** (…) Mission follows mastery.
